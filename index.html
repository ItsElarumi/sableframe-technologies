Common questions

Good to know.

A few practical details before you send an enquiry.

Is the £49 starter website offer final?

£49 is a proposed introductory price, not a confirmed quote for every project. Requirements, scope, final price and availability must be agreed before work begins.

Will my website work on phones?

The proposed starter package includes a responsive layout intended for desktop and mobile screens. The finished website should be checked on the target devices before it is considered complete.

Are hosting and a domain included?

No. Domains and paid hosting are not included. A simple static website may be hosted using a suitable free service, subject to that service's terms and limitations.

How do I send an enquiry?

The contact form prepares an email in your configured email application. It does not send the message automatically or store form details on this website.

Can you build software or automation too?

Software development, automation and quality testing are areas Sableframe is developing. Availability for work outside the listed website offer is not guaranteed, so ask first about a specific requirement.

Do you guarantee a delivery date?

No. The 5–7 working day target is an estimate after requirements and content are agreed and received. A timeline must be confirmed for each project before work begins.
